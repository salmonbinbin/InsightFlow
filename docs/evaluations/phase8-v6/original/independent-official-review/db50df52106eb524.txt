Turn camera uploads on or off for your Dropbox team
Updated Jan 24, 2025
In this article
The information in this article applies to certain types of admins on Dropbox Standard, Business, Advanced, Business Plus, and Enterprise.
Camera uploads is a feature that automatically uploads photos from your mobile device to Dropbox. If you’re a Dropbox team admin, you can turn camera uploads on or off for your team. To do so:
Log in to dropbox.com with your admin credentials.
Click Admin console.
Click Settings.
Under Content, click Device backups.
Toggle camera uploads to On or Off.
Click Save.
