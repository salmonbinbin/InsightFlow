Tell me about manually upload files or photos to OneDrive
How do I create folders in OneDrive for Android?
Help me create a new folder in the OneDrive for iOS app.
Use the OneDrive app to upload photos and videos from your device, create new folders, and move photos and videos from one OneDrive folder to another.
If you want to have your device's camera roll upload to OneDrive automatically, read how to Automatically save photos and videos from OneDrive on iOS.
Upload files and folders using the OneDrive for iOS app
In the OneDrive app, tap
OneDrive add icon
at the top of the screen.
Then tap to Take a photo, Scan a document, Create a folder or Upload a file or photo.
Note
OneDrive cannot upload photos optimized for iCloud. To disable optimization, tap Settings > iCloud > Photos and uncheck Optimize Storage.
Learn how to upload HEIF and HEVC photos and videos to OneDrive.
If you choose Take a Photo, your device's camera app opens. You might be asked if you want OneDrive to access your camera - tap Yes to allow your pictures to be saved in OneDrive.
If you tap Choose Existing, links appear to the Photos app on your device. Browse to the pictures you want to upload, select them, then on the top bar, tap Done.
Move files between folders in the OneDrive for iOS app
Select the files you want to move.
Tap
More icon in the OneDrive app for iOS
at the top of the screen, then tap Move. You can move folders into another folder, as well as individual files.
Your Files list appears. Tap the folder you want the files moved to, then tap Move.
Create a new folder in the OneDrive for iOS app
You can add folders in the Files view, or in any other folder in OneDrive.
Go to the location where you want to add a new folder,
Tap
OneDrive add icon
at the top of the screen and then tap Create a Folder.
Under Folder name, tap where it says "New folder" and type in the name you want for the folder, then tap Create.
