Manually upload files or photos with OneDrive for Android
Use the OneDrive app to upload photos and videos from your device, create new folders, and move photos and videos from one OneDrive folder to another.
If you want to have your device's camera roll upload to OneDrive automatically, read how to Automatically save photos and videos with OneDrive for Android.
How to upload files and folders in OneDrive for Android
In the OneDrive app, tap
OneDrive add icon
at the top of the screen.
Then tap to Take a photo, Scan a document, Create a folder or Upload a file or photo.
Note
If you choose Take a Photo, your device's camera app opens. You might be asked if you want OneDrive to access your camera - tap Yes to allow your pictures to be saved in OneDrive.
How to move files to another folder in OneDrive for Android
Select the files or folders you want to move.
Tap
OneDrive more icon
at the top of the screen, then tap
OneDrive move icon
to show your file list.
Tap the folder you want the files or folders moved to, then tap Move here.
How to create folders in OneDrive for Android
You can add folders in the Files view, or in any other folder in OneDrive.
Go to the location where you want to add a new folder.
Tap Add
OneDrive add icon
at the top of the screen, and then tap Create folder.
Type in the name you want for the folder, then tap OK.
