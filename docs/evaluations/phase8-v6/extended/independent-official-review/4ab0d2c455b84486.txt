Google Drive helps you keep all your files together. You can upload and share your files from any device, create new files, keep them organized, edit easily, and share them with others.
If you're new to Google Drive, this article will help you get started by covering the most important and useful things you can do, from learning how to use Drive and keeping your files organized to getting tips for using it on your computer, phone, or tablet.

To quickly find your important files or folders, you can add them to the "Starred" section.

Sign out of Google Drive
To sign out of Drive on Android, remove your Google Account from your device. To remove a Google Account:
On your Android device, open Settings Settings app.
Tap Users & accounts.
Select the account you want to remove.
Tap Remove account and then Remove account.

Use Split Screen with Google Drive
On an Android device with a large screen, you can open 2 instances of the Drive app side-by-side. For example, you can open a PDF on one side of your screen and scroll through your folders on the other screen.
To open 2 instances of the Drive app side-by-side:
If your device is more than 600 dpi and on Android 12 or up:
On your Android device, open Google Drive Google Drive icon.
On any Drive item, tap Menu More.
Select Open in split view.
If your device is on Android 7 or up:
On your Android device, open Google Drive Google Drive icon.
Enter split screen mode with another app.
On any Drive item, tap Menu More.
Select Open in other window.
To open 2 instances of the Drive app with 2 different accounts:
If your device is more than 600 dpi and on Android 12 or up:
On your Android device, open Google Drive Google Drive icon.
Next to a file, tap Menu More.
Select Open in split view.
At the top right of the new window, select your profile photo.
Switch your account.
If your device is on Android 7 or up:
On your Android device, open Google Drive Google Drive icon.
Enter split screen mode with another app.
On any Drive item, tap Menu More.
Select Open in other window.
At the top right of the new window, select your profile photo.
Switch your account.
