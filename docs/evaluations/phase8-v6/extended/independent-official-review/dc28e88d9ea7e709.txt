Use iCloud Drive on iCloud.com
With iCloud Drive on iCloud.com, you can access and share files stored in iCloud.
Learn how to do any of the following with iCloud Drive on iCloud.com:
Upload and download files
View files and folders
Organize files and folders
Share files and folders
Delete files
Recover deleted files
To access your iCloud Drive files and folders on other devices, see Set up iCloud Drive on all your devices.
Note: iCloud Drive on iCloud.com is available for collaboration for web-only accounts. The available apps and features may vary. Learn about apps and features available for web-only accounts.
